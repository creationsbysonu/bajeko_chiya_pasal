# Copyright & Content Governance Policy (COPYRIGHT.md)

## 1. Respect for Nepali Music Rights Holders
Bajeko Chiya Pasal exists as a cultural and educational platform celebrating Nepali musical heritage.
- **We do not host or store audio media files.**
- All musical playback is rendered solely via official, publicly accessible YouTube embeds permitted by uploaders and rights holders.
- Inclusion of an embed within Bajeko Chiya Pasal does not imply ownership, licensing, or commercial endorsement of the musical composition or sound recording.

## 2. Distinction of Metadata
All song records in our catalog clearly disclose:
- Verified Composer, Lyricist, Singer, and Arranger where known
- Official YouTube channel attribution and source URL
- Metadata verification status (`verified`, `community_submitted`, or `demo`)

## 3. Takedown & Moderation Procedure
If you are a copyright owner, musical artist, producer, or legal representative, and wish to have an embed removed or metadata corrected:

1. Use the built-in **Content & Takedown** form accessible from any page header or footer.
2. Alternatively, email **copyright@bajekochiyapasal.org** with:
   - The song title and artist.
   - The URL of the infringing or disputed content.
   - Evidence or declaration of rights ownership.
3. Upon receiving a notice:
   - A moderation ticket is instantly recorded in our audit log.
   - Our administrative team immediately reviews the complaint and can unpublish the song (`status='taken_down'`).
   - Content is not reinstated without proper verification.
